Frequently asked questions

Which AI model should I use to remove vocals?
BS-RoFormer gives the cleanest vocal/instrumental split (12.5 dB vocal SDR measured on the MUSDB18 test set). Mel-Band RoFormer can sound more natural on some voices. Use Demucs when you need drums, bass, guitar or piano as separate stems.
How long does a song take?
With an NVIDIA GPU and the default Fast speed, about 0.6–0.7 seconds per second of music: roughly 3 minutes for a 4–5 minute song. Max takes about 3× longer for a difference you can't hear. Without an NVIDIA GPU it runs on the processor and is much slower.
Are my songs uploaded to the internet?
No. In the Windows app everything runs on your own computer; your audio never leaves it. Uploads are deleted every time the app starts. Results are saved to Music\Mixlumo.
Which files can I use?
Almost any audio or video file: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG, AAC, MP4, MKV, MOV and more, up to 1 GB. You can save results as WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG or M4A.
Can I hear changes before I download?
Yes. Every uploaded song has a play button. On Pitch & Tempo the speed and pitch sliders change the sound live while it plays; Apply changes then renders the full-quality file.
“The AI engine stopped unexpectedly” — what now?
Usually the graphics card ran out of memory or its driver restarted. Close games or other AI apps and try again, or use the Fast speed or Demucs. The app keeps running; the next job starts a fresh AI engine.
